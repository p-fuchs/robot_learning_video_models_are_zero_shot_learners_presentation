Zero-shot learning

Zero-shot learning (or zero-shot prompting) refers to the amount of information you give to the model before enabling it to perform a task. We don't provide model an example or a list of steps to follow, we just ask the model to solve some issue. This is different from the one-shot (or few-shot) prompting in which we provide many examples for the model to be based on.
Prompting type Example
zero-shot
Classify the news-article into three categories: [Technology, Sport, Literature].
Article to classify: <content>
one-shot
Classify the news-article into three categories: [Technology, Sport, Literature].
Example:
Article [Classification: Technology]: <content>
Article to classify: <content>
few-shot
Classify the news-article into three categories: [Technology, Sport, Literature].
Examples:
Article [Classification: Technology]: <content>
Article [Classification: Sport]: <content>
Article [Classification: Literature]: <content>
Article to classify: <content>

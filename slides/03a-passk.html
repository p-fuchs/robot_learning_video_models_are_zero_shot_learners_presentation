• Concept: We are looking at ONE task (e.g., one specific maze).
  • The Setup: We generated 5 different attempts to solve this one maze.
  • The Result: Only the 3rd attempt actually solved it.
  • pass@1: If we randomly pick just one video from the 5, we have a 20% chance of holding the winner.
  • pass@5: If we get to keep all 5 videos, we are 100% sure we have the winner.
  • Final Metric: We average these probabilities across all 1,000 mazes in the dataset.

Understanding pass@k

The "Creative" Analogy

Video generation is like a Graphic Designer presenting logo options to a client.

The designer creates a batch of drafts. The client only needs to approve one.

Why this metric?
Standard accuracy asks: "Is the average draft good?"
pass@k asks: "Is the best draft good?"

Task
Given a scene with 1–9 animals, extract and line them up on white space, separated.
No categories or locations provided; zero-shot “tally”.
Data & prompt
Nano Banana generated 54 images (6 per count, 7 animal types).
Prompt: place the exact number of animals outdoors, non-overlapping.
Models: Veo 3 & Veo 2 via Vertex; 10 videos per sample.
